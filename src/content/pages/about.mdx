AsiaPicks is an Asia travel discovery and planning website for international travelers. Our first area of expertise is South Korea: Seoul, Busan, Jeju, Gyeongju and the trips between them. We add a place only when we can keep its guides accurate, so Korea is the only country covered today.

## What we publish

We publish planning guides that answer the questions first-time visitors ask: where to stay, how to get from the airport, which transport card to buy, which tours are worth booking and how many days each city needs. Each guide is built around one question and says the answer near the top, before the detail.

Guides take different shapes depending on the question. A procedural guide walks through a task in order, such as booking a KTX train. A comparison opens with our verdict and a table. A where-to-stay guide judges each area by who it suits and who it does not. Recommendations inside a guide are labelled "Our pick" so they are never confused with plain facts.

## Who writes AsiaPicks

Guides are written and signed by the AsiaPicks Editorial Team. The site is research-based curation: we read official and primary sources, compare what they say, and explain the result. We do not claim first-hand visits, and we do not publish invented reviews, invented travellers or invented authors.

## Where our information comes from

Practical facts come from the organisations that set them: the Korea Tourism Organization, Korail, AREX, the Seoul Metropolitan Government, Busan Transportation Corporation, the Korea Heritage Service (which runs the royal palaces), Hallasan National Park, city and district offices, and the Korea Meteorological Administration for climate figures. Each guide lists its sources at the foot of the page with the publisher and the date each one was checked.

Fares, entry rules and admission fees that appear on more than one guide are stored once in a shared fact record with their source. When one of those values changes, every guide that quotes it changes in the same update, so two of our pages never disagree about the same fare.

## How the dates on a guide work

The "Updated" date at the top of a guide changes only when something on the page changes: a price, a rule, an opening time or the advice itself. Re-checking a source and finding nothing different moves that source's "checked" date, not the "Updated" date. You can read the full routine in our [editorial policy](/editorial-policy).

## How our guides are made

We use AI tools to help research and draft, and a person reviews every page before it is published. Drafts wait for approval, and nothing goes live without it. Photos of real places come from the Korea Tourism Organization's photo library or Wikimedia Commons, and each one is credited with its author and licence on the page.

## How we make money

Some pages link to booking partners: Viator, Creatrip, Trip.com and Agoda. If you book through those links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. A commission never decides what a guide says or which option we pick. Read our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure) and [how we choose recommendations](/how-we-choose).

## Tell us when something is wrong

Prices and rules in Korea change, sometimes with little notice. If a guide no longer matches what you found, tell us through the [contact page](/contact) with the page and the detail, and we will check it against the official source.
