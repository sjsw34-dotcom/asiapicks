## How many days do you need?

Our pick for a first trip is five days in Seoul, or eight to ten days to include Busan or Jeju. With two weeks, add Gyeongju and a slower pace. Start with the [South Korea travel guide](/korea) to choose your route.

## Which month should you pick?

Our pick is October. Seoul's average low and high are 9.9–20.1°C (50–68°F), with about 50mm of rain compared with 415mm in July. April is another mild option. These are 1991–2020 climate averages, not a forecast; use the [Seoul guide](/korea/seoul) to plan your stay.

## Do you need a visa or a K-ETA?

Most visitors need one form rather than two. Nationals of eligible visa-free countries and regions are exempt from the K-ETA until 31 December 2026, and not holding a K-ETA is exactly what makes the free e-Arrival Card mandatory, submitted in the three days before you arrive. Holding a K-ETA exempts you from the card, and so does a Korean residence card. The [official e-Arrival Card guide](https://www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr/portal/guide/eacTargetGuide.do?locale=E) lists who must declare, the [official K-ETA site](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/) holds the country list, and [K-ETA or e-Arrival Card](/korea/k-eta-e-arrival-card) works through which applies to you.

## What does getting around cost?

A Seoul subway ride starts at KRW 1,550 (Korean won) on a transit card. Busan's subway costs KRW 1,600 or 1,800 depending on the section. A T-money card works in both cities. Read the [Seoul fare and transfer guide](/korea/seoul/subway-fares-and-transfers) for the details.
