Jeju is a ring. Nothing is very far in kilometres, but the sights are distributed around the coast and across the interior rather than gathered into a walkable centre.

That makes transport the decision the whole trip hangs on, and it is worth taking before booking rather than after arriving. These guides set out what each option actually reaches.
