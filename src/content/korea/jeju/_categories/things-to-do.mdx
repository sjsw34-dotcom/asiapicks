Much of what draws people to Jeju is outdoors, which means rules and weather matter more than opening hours. The summit of Hallasan needs a booking, trails close in heavy rain or snow, and the island's bus network decides how much fits in a day.

Each guide states those conditions up front, with the source and the date it was checked.
