Jeju looks small on a map and is not small on a bus. The airport and most flights sit in Jeju City on the north coast, while the waterfalls, the Olle trails and the sunrise peak are spread around the rest of the island.

These guides compare the bases by who they suit and what they cost in travel time, and say plainly where the answer changes if you are driving.
