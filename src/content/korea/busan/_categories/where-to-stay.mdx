Busan is linear. The city runs along the coast and over hills rather than spreading from one centre, so two areas that look close on a map can be a long ride apart.

That shape has a consequence Seoul does not: the subway charges by section, so where you sleep can change what a daily journey costs, not just how long it takes. These guides compare areas on both.
