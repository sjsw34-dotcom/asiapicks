Busan is long rather than wide. The old port around Busan Station, the shopping streets of Seomyeon and the beaches and coastal temples to the east sit far enough apart that the order of a day matters more than the number of sights in it.

These itineraries keep each day in one part of the city, say how to get from stop to stop, and leave room for the ride back to the hotel.
