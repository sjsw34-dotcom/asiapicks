Adult means 19 and over at Lotte World and 13 and over at Everland, so teenagers pay adult prices at Everland. Prices and hours are as both parks listed them when we checked on 15 October 2026.

## Why is Lotte World the easier day?

Because it costs almost nothing in time. Lotte World Adventure is at exit 4 of Jamsil Station, on Lines 2 and 8, so it fits around a morning elsewhere in Seoul for the price of a subway fare, from {{seoul-subway-fare-card}} on a transit card. The rules for changing lines are in [Seoul subway fares and transfers](/korea/seoul/subway-fares-and-transfers).

The weather rarely ends a visit. Lotte World describes the Adventure section as enclosed by a huge glass dome, with rides, parades and shows that run whatever the weather, plus the Underland level in the basement. Only Magic Island, reached by a bridge from the second floor, sits outdoors on Seokchon Lake. That is where Atlantis, which launches at 72km/h, Comet Express and the Gyro rides are. Lotte World says some Magic Island rides stop below 0°C, above 30°C, in winds of 7 to 14m/s or more, or in rain of 0.2mm or more.

The hours suit a late start. On the October dates we checked, the park opened at 10am and closed at 9pm on weekdays and 10pm on Fridays and Saturdays, and ticket sales and entry end an hour before closing. The After4 park pass, 52,000 won for adults entering from 4pm, makes an evening visit cheaper.

Our pick for most visitors is Lotte World, on a weekday evening or a rainy day, because it asks the least of a short trip.

## When is Everland worth the trip?

When you want what only Everland has, and you can give it a whole day. Everland is an outdoor park in Yongin, south of Seoul, with three things Lotte World lacks.

- **monimo RUSH**, the wooden coaster that opened as T Express and was renamed on 27 April 2026. Everland lists a top speed of 104km/h and a 77-degree drop. Riders must be 130 to 195cm tall, and on Mondays it opens at 1pm after a weekly inspection.
- **Safari World: The Wild**, the safari ride, which ran from 9:50am to 5pm on the days we checked.
- **Panda World**, the giant panda house, open from 10am to 5:30pm on the days we checked.

Amazon Express, the rapids ride, ran from 9:30am to 8:30pm. The animal attractions close by early evening, so an Everland day starts at opening, not after lunch.

Everland was open from 10am to 10pm every day from 15 to 22 October 2026. It is all outdoors, and Everland warns that rides can stop in heavy rain, strong wind or low temperatures; monimo RUSH stops in lightning and below minus 5°C. Check the forecast before you commit the day.

## How do you get to Everland from Seoul?

By rail, ride the Suin-Bundang Line to Giheung Station and change to the Everline, a light rail line that runs from 5:30am to 11:30pm. At its last stop, a free shuttle bus runs to Everland every 5 to 10 minutes, from 7:30am until an hour after the park closes.

Three Seoul city buses also run to Everland: 5002 from Sinnonhyeon, Gangnam and Yangjae; 4105 from Myeongdong, Seoul Station and Sungnyemun; and 5700 from Gangbyeon, Jamsil, Songpa and Suseo stations. Everland lists the routes but not the journey times.

Tour companies also run reserved shuttle coaches from central Seoul. Everland names Daesung Tour and World Tour for Seoul routes and sends bookings and questions to them; it does not publish their fares, so check with the operator.

## What do tickets cost, and are there discounts for visitors?

At the gate, Lotte World Adventure is {{lotte-world-park-pass-adult}} for an adult park pass, or {{lotte-world-full-day-pass-adult}} for the full-day pass that adds the Folk Museum, which stops admitting visitors at 6pm. Youth tickets (13 to 18) are 56,000 won and children's (36 months to 12) 49,000 won for the park pass.

Everland prices its day pass by month. For adults and youths it is 71,000 won in October 2026, 65,000 won in November and 55,000 won in December; children (36 months to 12) and over-65s pay 61,000, 55,000 and 45,000 won. Children under 36 months enter free.

Neither park lists a general foreigner discount on its own price page. Everland's page says its preferential rates do not apply to foreigners unless they hold a Korean alien registration card. The cheaper route for visitors is a reseller: when we checked on 15 October 2026, Creatrip listed the Lotte World full-day pass from US$32.93 and the Everland full-day pass from US$36.31, both date-specific. The Lotte World ticket cannot be cancelled or changed after payment.

## What is on for Halloween?

Both parks run Halloween seasons into November. Lotte World's Dark Fantasy Festival parade runs from 12 September to 15 November 2026, at 8:20pm on the Adventure first-floor parade route, and lasts 25 minutes. Everland's Blood City Zero runs from 12 September to 22 November 2026.

For a theme park on its own, Lotte World's indoor parade is the easier evening; for a full-day Halloween outing, Everland's season runs a week longer.
