Seoul rewards a day out, and the rail network makes more of the country reachable than the map suggests. The question is rarely whether somewhere is worth seeing and usually whether it is worth the hours in transit.

These guides give the door-to-door time first, then what the day actually contains. Where a destination can only be visited on an organised tour, that is stated plainly rather than left for you to discover at the booking stage.
