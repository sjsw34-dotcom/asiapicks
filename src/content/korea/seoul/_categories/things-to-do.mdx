Seoul's best-known sights come with rules that guides often skip. Gyeongbokgung closes on Tuesdays while Changdeokgung closes on Mondays, hanbok gets you into the main palaces free only if it meets a published standard, and parts of Bukchon are closed to tourists outside set hours.

These guides put those rules first, with the source and the date each one was checked.
