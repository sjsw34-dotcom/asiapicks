Seoul's sights are spread across a large city, and its palaces do not share a closing day. A plan that ignores either loses hours to a locked gate or a needless ride from one side of the river to the other.

These itineraries are ordered by neighbourhood so each day stays in one part of the city, with the travel between stops written into the text and the closing days stated up front.
