Days in Seoul are spent wherever the sights are, and the subway makes that workable from most central bases. Evenings are spent near the hotel, which is the part the neighbourhood actually decides.

These guides compare areas on that basis rather than on hotel quality: how connected each one is, what it is like to walk back through at night, and who each suits. Recommendations are labelled as ours and kept separate from the booking links.
