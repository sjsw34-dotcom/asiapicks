Myeongdong sits in the middle of old Seoul, with department stores along Euljiro to the north and Namsan rising to the south. Its streets are given over to cosmetics, fashion and duty-free shopping, and in the evening the main lanes fill with food stalls.

It is a district that works best when you know what it is for. The guides below cover what Myeongdong does well, where it falls short, and how it compares with the other areas first-time visitors weigh up as a base.
