Seongsu grew up around small factories and shoe workshops. Many of those buildings now hold cafes, design shops and short-lived brand pop-ups, which is why the neighbourhood draws younger visitors who have already done the palaces.

It is a place to wander rather than to tick off. The guides below explain how to reach it, how the pop-up scene works and how it fits into a day in Seoul.
