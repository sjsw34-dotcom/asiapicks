Korea's cities run separate transport networks with separate fares, but the card that pays for them is national. A T-money card bought in Seoul works on Busan's subway and buses, and in taxis in both.

The decisions that remain are about price. A Seoul pass can undercut per-ride fares for a stay built around the capital, and a card that doubles as a shopping balance suits some travellers more than others. Every fare and fee in these guides carries a source and the date it was checked.
