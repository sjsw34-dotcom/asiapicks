Korea's seasons are sharp enough to decide when a trip should happen. Autumn colour moves down the country from the northern mountains to Jeju over about six weeks, so the same October week can be past peak in one place and weeks away in another.

These guides work out timing from published forecasts and official visitor information, and say plainly when a date is a forecast rather than a fixed schedule.
